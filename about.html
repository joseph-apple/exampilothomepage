Our Story

Hi, I'm Joseph

I'm a Year 11 student on a mission to revolutionize how students revise. During my own exam journey, I realized how frustrating it was to find a revision tool that actually respects your time and attention. Most platforms are cluttered with ads and distracting features that pull you away from what really matters—your studies.

That's why I created ExamPilot. I wanted to build something completely different: a clean, focused revision organizer with zero advertisements and no hidden costs. Something designed by a student, for students, that puts learning first and everything else second.

My goal is simple but ambitious—to make revision smarter, less stressful, and genuinely effective for GCSE and A-Level students everywhere. Because every student deserves access to powerful tools without the nonsense getting in the way.

The ExamPilot Promise

ExamPilot will always be free and always be ad-free. No hidden pricing, no paid tiers, no tricks. Just a simple, honest tool built to help you revise effectively and organize your exam preparation without distractions.

Every feature is designed with one thing in mind: helping you focus on what matters. Whether you're planning your revision timetable, tracking your progress, or preparing for your exams, ExamPilot is here to make it easier.
